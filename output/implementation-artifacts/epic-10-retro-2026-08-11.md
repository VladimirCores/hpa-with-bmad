## Successes

1. **Deferral system closed its own backlog.** Every finding 10-1's two review rounds deferred was written to `deferred-work.md`, and 10-2 was built to close them: `/gql`+`/telemetry` shadowing, JWKS host, key isolation, harbor build, envSlug drift, test robustness — six for six resolved.
2. **Mutation-probe reviews were the highest-leverage technique.** In-memory probes in the 10-2 checkpoint review exposed real coverage holes that static reading missed (Dig-in 1 path-ownership, Dig-in 2 harbor drift, Dig-in 4 dev-slug window).
3. **Structure-aware P0 suites.** `test_no_route_shadowing`, `_UniqueKeyLoader` duplicate-key rejection, overlay kustomization resolution, effective parentRef resolution, strict `main()` tuple guards — the suite now catches the bug class the first review rounds missed.
4. **Offline/GitOps-safe discipline held** end to end across both stories.
5. **Course correction handled cleanly.** Task 2.4's harbor regression was reconciled to AC 4 intent (values source is not a kustomize resource; `harbor-values` ConfigMap is the source) rather than reverted.

## Challenges & Growth Areas

1. **Cross-system drift.** Dropping `harbor-values.yaml` from kustomize `resources` silently broke `install_harbor_dev.py --check` — two representations of one config (kustomize tree vs installer scripts) drifted. Fixed with a parsed-equality drift guard, but the fragility is systemic.
2. **Toolchain friction.** No `kustomize` binary (pure-Python structural validator became the always-on path), exhausted `/tmp` disk quota, and `--load-restrictor=LoadRestrictionsNone` required for all builds.
3. **Live-cluster-dependent residue.** InfisicalSecret CRD does not reconcile (no operator), JWT `audiences`/`forwardJWT` + live JWKS fetch unverifiable offline (P0-008).
4. **Latent route ambiguity.** Two native-auth UI routes share a `*.hpdc.local` PathPrefix `/` catch-all; `argocd.hpdc.local/` / `backstage.hpdc.local/` route nondeterministically (Dig-in 3, accept-and-defer).

## Key Insights

1. Two representations of one fact (kustomize resources vs installer expectations, ConfigMap embed vs on-disk file) will always drift until a parity guard enforces equality.
2. Structure-aware parsing beats regex/substring for config auditing.
3. A deferral without an owner and a written target is just a leak — `deferred-work.md` drove an entire follow-up story.
4. Mutation probes are the cheapest way to prove a check can fail.
