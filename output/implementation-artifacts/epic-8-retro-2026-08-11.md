## Epic Summary & Metrics

- **Completion:** 3/3 stories (100%)
- **Scope:** Cilium ClusterMesh over WireGuard VPN, independent regional data stores with no default cross-region replication, central hub querying regional APIs with region-scoped auth (hub stores no regional data)
- **Constraint honored:** offline/GitOps-safe — no internet, no live cluster

## Successes

1. **Coherent story set.** The three contracts are mutually consistent — encrypted cross-cluster discovery (ClusterMesh), no-replication-by-default (sovereignty), and read-only aggregate visibility (hub stores nothing). A real federation story, not three islands.
2. **Data-flow invariants encoded in ACs.** "Hub does not store regional data", "replication requires explicit config" — hard invariants, not cosmetic requirements.

## Challenges & Growth Areas

1. **Offline validation ceiling.** Cross-region WireGuard discovery/encryption is a live-network-path feature; manifest-contract tests cannot prove the tunnel works. Live-cluster verification (P0-008) remains the only real proof.
2. **Absence-of-config invariants.** Sovereignty is enforced by the *absence* of replication config — cheap to violate silently when someone later adds a replication job.

## Key Insights

1. Coherent, mutually-agreeing story sets ship cleaner than independent features.
2. Sovereignty invariants are exactly the class of "absence of config" that needs a guard — same lesson as Epic 10's parity guards.
